Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

LuluCam is a camera. This policy describes what it does with your data, and the short version is: it keeps it on your phone.

What LuluCam collects

Nothing is sent anywhere. LuluCam makes exactly one kind of network request, and it is Apple's: the App Store call that lists the subscription prices and completes a purchase. There is no LuluCam server, no account, no sign-in, and no analytics service. Nothing you photograph, and nothing about how you use the app, leaves your device.

Your photographs

Photographs you take are written to your own Photo Library and nowhere else. LuluCam has no copy of them and no way to send one.

Location is removed by default. When LuluCam saves a photograph it does not write GPS coordinates into it. This is on out of the box; the switch is Strip location on the You tab, and turning it off is the only way a LuluCam photograph carries a location.

If Keep original is on — also the default — the unedited frame is saved beside the graded one, in the same library, under the same rules.

The camera and the Director

LuluCam analyses the live camera feed to score your pose, find the horizon, check whether eyes are open and judge the light. All of this runs on your device, using Apple's Vision framework. Camera frames are examined in memory and discarded; they are not stored, and they are not transmitted.

  • Your photographs, in your Photo Library.
  • A small index of the photographs LuluCam took, so the Library tab can show
  • Your settings and your onboarding answers.
  • A short in-memory record of six kinds of app event — a capture completed, a

Deleting LuluCam removes all of it except the photographs, which are yours and stay in your Photo Library.

Permissions

  • Camera — required to take photographs.
  • Photo Library (add only) — required to save them.

LuluCam does not ask for contacts, location services, microphone, or the ability to read your existing photo library beyond the pictures it wrote.

Purchases

LuluCam Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment and tells LuluCam only whether a subscription is currently active. LuluCam never sees your name, your email address, or your payment details, and does not receive a purchase history.

Children

LuluCam is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship in an app update and the date at the top will change.
